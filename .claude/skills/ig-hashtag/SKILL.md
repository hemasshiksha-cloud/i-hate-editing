# IG Hashtag & Keywords

## Rule: exactly 5 hashtags
2 niche/brand + 2 location/community + 1 topic of the post.
- Brand/niche: #Vriksh #HerbalSanitaryNapkins #PeriodCare #MenstrualHealth #OrganicPads
- UAE: #DubaiSmallBusiness #UAEBrand #SmallBusinessUAE #DubaiMoms
- India: #ChennaiStartup #MadeInIndia #WomenEntrepreneursIndia
- Community: #WomenInBusiness #Mompreneur #ResellerOpportunity
Rotate sets; never use banned or irrelevant giant tags (#love, #instagood).

## Keywords (more important than hashtags now)
- Caption keyword line: 3 to 5 phrases, " | " separated.
- Alt text with 2 keywords. Name field with the main keyword.
- Full list as ONE comma-separated line for YouTube tags (Hema's preferred format).
Keep a per-post log of which set was used so results can be compared in `/ig-stats`.

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
