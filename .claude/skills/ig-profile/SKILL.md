# IG Profile

## Score out of 100
| Area | Points | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Name field (searchable) | 15 | Keyword, not just the brand: "Vriksh · Herbal Sanitary Napkins" |
| Bio line 1: who it's for + promise | 15 | Clear in 3 seconds |
| Bio proof | 10 | A milestone (first corporate order, award) |
| Bio CTA + link | 15 | One action: shop / become a reseller / WhatsApp |
| Profile photo | 5 | Logo readable at tiny size |
| Highlights | 15 | 4 to 6: Shop, Reviews, Resellers, Corporate, How to use, About |
| Pinned posts (3) | 15 | Best proof reel, product explainer, founder story |
| Grid consistency | 10 | Recognisable covers and colours |

## Output
Score table with notes, then copy-ready: name field, 3 bio options (150 chars max),
link-in-bio plan (Linktree-style list), highlight names + cover idea, which 3 posts to pin.
Ask for a screenshot of the profile if none was shared.

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
