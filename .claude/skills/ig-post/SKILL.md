# IG Post

## Output
1. **Hook** (first line, under 15 words). Pick the strongest of 3 options and show all 3.
   Formats: POV, "Nobody tells you...", a surprising detail, a question, before/after.
2. **Caption**: 3 to 6 short lines of story or value, then one product line, then
   one engagement prompt (tag someone / pick A or B / share yours).
3. **CTA**: comment keyword (VRIKSH / RESELLER / OFFICE) or DM.
4. **5 hashtags** (see `/ig-hashtag`).
5. **Keyword line**: 3 to 5 search phrases separated by " | ", after ". . ." spacers.
6. **Alt text**: one sentence, visuals + 2 keywords.
7. **First comment** to pin (a question or extra tip that starts conversation).

For reels, `/vriksh-caption` adds YouTube Shorts title and tags.

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
