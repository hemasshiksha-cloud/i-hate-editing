# IG Comment (outbound engagement)

## Method
1. Ask for (or read) the post: who posted, what it says, Hema's goal (network, collab, customers, agency clients).
2. Write 3 options, each **specific to the post** (mention one real detail), 1 to 3 sentences:
   - **Add value**: a tip or experience from Vriksh / teaching / founder life.
   - **Ask**: a thoughtful question the creator will want to answer.
   - **Celebrate**: genuine praise + why it matters to Hema.
3. Never pitch in a comment, never drop links, never "Nice post! Check my page".
4. Max 2 emojis. Sound like a person, not a brand.

## Daily routine (15 min)
10 comments/day: 4 on niche creators (period care, wellness), 3 on UAE/Chennai small
businesses, 3 on potential agency clients. Reply to anyone who replies back.

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
