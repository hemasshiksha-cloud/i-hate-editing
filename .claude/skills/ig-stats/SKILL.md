# IG Stats

## Inputs
Screenshots or numbers from Instagram Insights (Claude cannot log in to Instagram).
Ask for: reach, views, avg watch time, % watched to end, likes, comments, shares, saves,
follows, profile visits, link taps, DMs from the post.

## Read it in this order
1. **Hook**: views from non-followers + 3-second hold. Low = fix the first 2 seconds.
2. **Retention**: avg watch time vs length. Low = cut slower; tighten the middle.
3. **Value**: saves + shares per 1,000 views (shares = reach engine, saves = trust).
4. **Business**: profile visits, follows, link taps, DMs, orders. This is what matters most.

## Output
Plain-language summary (3 bullets), a simple table of the numbers, 3 actions for next week,
and one A/B test (e.g. same reel, two hooks). Add the result to the Video log in
VRIKSH_STUDIO.md so future style/sound choices learn from what performed.
Never invent a benchmark number; say "compare with your last 5 posts".

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
