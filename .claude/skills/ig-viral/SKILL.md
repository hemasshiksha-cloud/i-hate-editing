# IG Viral

## Inputs
Hema shares viral reels (screenshots/links/descriptions) or names accounts; Claude cannot
browse Instagram itself, so work from what she shares plus web research where available.

## Breakdown per reel
Hook (first 2 s, words + visual) · format · length · pacing · on-screen text style ·
emotion (curiosity, relatability, pride, surprise) · CTA · why it got shared/saved.

## Output
1. **Swipe file** table: format, why it works, Vriksh version (hook + 3-beat outline).
2. **5 hooks** adapted to Vriksh, scored on curiosity, clarity, relevance (out of 10).
3. Pick the best one and offer to script it (`/ig-reel`) and edit it in a new studio style.
Never copy someone's content, audio or exact words; copy the mechanism, not the material.

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
