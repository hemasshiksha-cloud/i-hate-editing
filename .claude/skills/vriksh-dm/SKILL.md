# Vriksh DM

Goal: every comment gets a public reply within the hour, every interested person
gets a DM, every DM moves one step closer to an order on WhatsApp.

Brand facts: Vriksh, Made For Her. Herbal and plant-based sanitary napkins, anion
disposable period panty. Delivers across the UAE & India.
`@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins`.

**Never invent prices, delivery times or stock.** Use `[Price] AED`, `[delivery area / timeline]`,
`[payment method]`, `[WhatsApp number]` placeholders unless Hema gave the real values.
Never write em dashes. Each message goes in its own copy box.

## 1. Public comment replies (rotate, keep under 20 words)

- `Yay, thank you! 💚 Check your DMs, we've sent you all the details 🌿`
- `So happy you asked! 🥹 Just sent you a message 💚`
- `Thank you lovely! 🌿 Details are in your inbox now ✨`
- Compliment or emotional comment: thank them and ask a question back, so the thread keeps going.
- Negative or doubtful comment: calm, kind, one fact, invite to DM. Never argue in public.

## 2. DM flow

1. **Welcome + offer** (sent first or by automation): thank them, one line on what
   Vriksh is, 3 sparkle bullets (herbal & plant-based napkins, soft breathable comfort,
   anion period panty), "We deliver across the UAE & India", then ONE question:
   "Would you like to try our pads, the period panty, or both?"
2. **Details**: product, `[Price] AED`, delivery, payment, then ask for name, phone and address.
3. **Confirm**: repeat the order back, thank them, set the delivery expectation.
4. **After delivery (3 to 5 days later)**: ask how it felt, ask for a review or a story tag, offer a reorder.
5. **Silent after 24 h**: ONE gentle nudge, never more:
   `Hi again! 😊 Just checking if you had any questions about Vriksh 🌿 Happy to help anytime 💚`

## 3. Common questions (answer honestly, no medical claims)

- Price / offers: give the real price or ask Hema. Bundles beat discounts.
- "Is it safe for sensitive skin?": describe materials (plant-based, soft cotton-feel top
  layer, breathable) and suggest trying one pack. Never promise "no rashes" or health outcomes.
- India delivery, bulk/school orders, gifting and corporate goodie bags: welcome it, ask quantity and date, move to WhatsApp or email.
- Collab/influencer requests: thank them, ask for their profile and audience location, say Hema will review.

## 4. When Instagram blocks the DM

The person limits messages from accounts they don't follow. In order:
1. Use **Send private reply** on their comment (Business or Creator accounts only).
2. Reply publicly asking them to DM "Hi": `Instagram isn't letting me message you directly 🥺 Could you send us a quick DM with "Hi"? I'll share everything right away 🌿`
3. Follow them, then DM.
4. Share WhatsApp: `https://wa.me/<number>?text=Hi%20Vriksh,%20I%20want%20to%20order`

## 5. Automation (official tools only)

- **ManyChat** (Meta partner, free up to 1,000 contacts): template "Auto-DM links from
  comments", any post, keywords `VRIKSH, vriksh, price, order, interested`, public reply
  on (rotate the 3 lines above), DM = welcome message plus 2 buttons (See Products, Order on WhatsApp).
  Needs a Business account and "Allow access to messages" switched on.
- Instagram **Saved replies** (shortcut `vriksh`) and **FAQ buttons**; Meta Business Suite **Instant reply**.
- WhatsApp Business **greeting** and **away** messages, and a **catalogue** with prices.
- Never use unofficial auto-DM apps; they risk account restrictions.
