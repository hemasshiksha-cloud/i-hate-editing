# IG Story

## Sequence (5 to 8 frames)
1. **Hook frame**: face or product + bold question.
2. **Context**: what's happening today.
3. **Interactive**: poll or quiz (easy tap = reach).
4. **Value or behind the scenes**.
5. **Proof**: review, order photo, delivery.
6. **Question sticker** ("ask me anything about...").
7. **CTA**: link sticker (WhatsApp/shop) or "DM VRIKSH".

## Output per frame
Visual (what to film/photograph) · text on screen (max 12 words) · sticker + exact wording · music mood.
Share every new reel to Stories with a "new reel" sticker. Save best frames to Highlights (`/ig-profile`).

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
