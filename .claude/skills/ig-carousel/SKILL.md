# IG Carousel

## Structure (7 to 10 slides, 1080x1350)
1. **Cover**: bold promise or question, max 8 words + a visual hook.
2. **Problem / why it matters** (relatable).
3 to 7. **One idea per slide**, max 25 words, a number or icon each.
8. **Proof**: Vriksh moment, review or milestone.
9. **Summary / save this**.
10. **CTA**: comment keyword, follow, share with a friend.

## Output
Slide table (headline, body, visual note), caption via `/ig-post`, and design notes
matching a studio style. Offer to render the slides as PNGs using the handmade kit
(`/handmade-edit` paper, tape, labels) or Canva (Hema's templates via the Canva connector).
Good Vriksh topics: herbal vs regular pads, how to choose pad size, period kit for work,
myths, how to become a reseller.

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
