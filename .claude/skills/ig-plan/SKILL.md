# IG Plan

## Weekly rhythm (adjust to Hema's time)
| Day | Format | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Carousel | Education: period care tips, herbal vs regular pads |
| Tue | Reel | Behind the scenes / founder story |
| Wed | Stories x5 | Poll, Q&A, product use |
| Thu | Reel | Proof: orders, deliveries, events, reviews |
| Fri | Post or carousel | Community: women in business, resellers |
| Sat | Reel (trend or fun) | Relatable period moments (tasteful) |
| Sun | Stories | Week recap + reseller CTA |

## Output
A table: date, format, hook, idea, studio style + sound (rotate, never repeat back-to-back; see VRIKSH_STUDIO.md),
CTA keyword, best time (UAE Tue to Thu 7:30 to 9 pm GST; India 7 to 9 pm IST).
Plus daily engagement tasks (`/ig-comment` 10/day) and one experiment for the week.
Mark which ideas need new footage and give Hema a shot list.

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
