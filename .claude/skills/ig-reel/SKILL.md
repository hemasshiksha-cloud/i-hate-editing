# IG Reel (pre-production)

## Output
1. **3 hooks** scored /10 (curiosity, clarity, scroll-stop visual). Recommend one.
2. **Beat sheet** (15 to 40 s):
| Time | Visual / shot | Voice or text on screen | Sound moment |
3. **Script** (if speaking): short spoken lines, natural Tanglish/English as Hema prefers.
4. **Shot list**: every clip/photo to capture, with framing tips (vertical, bright light, steady, 3 s per shot minimum, extra B-roll of hands and product).
5. **Studio pick**: which style + sound from VRIKSH_STUDIO.md (different from the last video).
6. CTA keyword and caption angle.

After filming, edit with `/handmade-edit` or `/vriksh-reel` (or the talking-head pipeline).

## Before you start
Read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md` (brand facts, Hema's rules, video log). Default brand is
Vriksh (@vrikshherbalsanitarynapkins, UAE & India); for an agency client, ask for
or reuse their brand notes first.

## Always
- Copy-ready output: each piece in its own code box.
- No em dashes. No medical claims ("rash-free", "cures"). Phone numbers in captions only.
- Never invent numbers (followers, sales, reach). Use what Hema gives or leave a [placeholder].
- Voice: warm, honest, feminine, a little playful; short lines; emojis as punctuation.
