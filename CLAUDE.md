# Project notes for Claude

This project is Hema's video studio for Vriksh (herbal sanitary napkins) and her future agency.

**Before any video, caption or DM task, read `VRIKSH_STUDIO.md`.** It holds the skills,
the style and sound libraries, the video log and Hema's rules.

Key rules:
- Every new video uses a style and a sound that differ from the previous video (check the Video log). Add a log row when a video is delivered.
- Never change a video the log marks as "Do not change".
- No em dashes. Phone numbers in captions only. HD footage only. Blur private details. Official Vriksh logo on end cards.
